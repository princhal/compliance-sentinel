Appendix B: method

Regulatory texts were fetched from their official sources, split into one chunk per article paragraph, and turned into structured obligations. Each obligation has a category that maps to static checks. For every category, the agent combined static check hits with hybrid code search, assessed each piece of evidence against the obligation, and sent every violation through a second review. Findings below the confidence threshold are marked for human review. Code locations always come from the repository itself, never from model output.

This report supports a legal and engineering review. It is not legal advice. Bundled regulation excerpts used in demos must be checked against the official texts.
